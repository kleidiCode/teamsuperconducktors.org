Chapter 03 · The crew

Five squads.
One shared duck obsession.

Nine of us (six girls and three boys) in five specialized squads. From CAD design to sponsor pitches, every squad works with the others to ensure a cohesive result. Right now that means a prototype base on mecanum wheels, a crash course in vectoring and OnBot Java, and a fundraiser that builds websites for local businesses.

Mechanical Design Squad M.D.S.

Owns the physical architecture: chassis, drivetrain, and manipulators. Right now that means our prototype base on mecanum wheels. Builds 3D CAD assemblies in Onshape, prototypes scoring mechanisms, and produces the technical diagrams and CAD documentation for the Engineering Portfolio.

Electrical Design & Assembly Squad E.D.A.S.

Brings the robot to life: wiring, sensor mounting, battery management, and motor controllers. Handles physical assembly, color-coded wiring harnesses, IMU and odometry pod integration, and ensures every electrical connection is competition-ready.

Autonomous Squad A.S.

Owns the first 30 seconds of every match. Programs AprilTag-based computer vision, IMU heading correction, odometry, and pathfinding (FTC RoadRunner). Scores points before a human ever touches the controller.

TeleOps & Driving Squad T.D.S.

Owns the 2-minute TeleOp period. Works out the mecanum vectoring that lets a driver drive, strafe, and turn at the same time, programs custom gamepad mappings (asymmetric stick layouts, one-button score/intake macros), trains primary and backup drivers, and builds manual fallbacks for every macro. Speed and intent over fancy buttons.
