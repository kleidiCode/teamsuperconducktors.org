Websites for local businesses

Run a local business? We'll build your website.

We build new websites and update existing ones for local businesses. Instead of a fee, we ask you to donate any amount you think is reasonable to our team. It goes toward our robot and our season.

See it before you decide

We build a working prototype of your site first, tested at phone width as we go. You look at it, tell us what's off, and decide from there.

Your words, word for word

We carry your existing copy over exactly as you wrote it. Anything new we add is yours to approve or cut.

Set up for search engines and AI

Clear page titles and descriptions, image descriptions, and structured data that spell out who you are, what you offer, and how to reach you: the details search engines and AI assistants read.

Yours to keep

You own the site and its files. No subscription, nothing locked to us, and you decide what goes live.

How it works

  1. Tell us about your business. What you do, and what your site needs.
  2. We build a prototype. A working version you can click through.
  3. You tell us what to change. We make the changes and check back in.
  4. You decide what goes live. It's your site, on your schedule.
